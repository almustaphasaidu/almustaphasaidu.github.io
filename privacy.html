Privacy policy

Anas Yahuza Islamic Companion · Updated 11 November 2026

Local data and accounts

No account is required. Settings, bookmarks, notes, reading progress, collections, downloaded content and pending feedback are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

Location and permissions

Prayer and Qibla request foreground location access when you open those features. Their location-dependent content is hidden until access is granted. No background location permission is requested. Other offline content remains usable without location. You can change permissions in Android Settings.

Notification permission and alarm access support optional prayer and reminder notifications. Foreground media services support audio and downloads. The app does not request microphone, contacts or personal photo access.

External services

  • Aladhan: prayer cross-checks and date metadata; requests can include prayer coordinates and your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap: Qibla map tiles; tile coordinates and your IP address are transmitted.
  • alquran.cloud: optional translations and tafsir by verse number.
  • archive.org: optional dua and ruqyah audio streams and downloads.
  • FormSubmit: feedback messages, optional reply email and submission time are sent over HTTPS to the developer's feedback inbox. Offline messages remain queued until delivery. Reopen the app to retry after it has been terminated.
  • Google Play: update availability and delivery for Play-installed versions.

Advertising

This app does not ask for an age group or birth date and stores no age answer. Every ad request uses Google's teen age treatment, so teens and adults receive the same restricted, non-personalized treatment. UMP still obtains any consent Google legally requires, and no religious content is locked behind an ad or an age answer.

Selected navigation and information screens use Google AdMob. Quran readers and players, prayer, Qibla and worship tools remain ad-free. We request non-personalized ads and a maximum ad content rating of G. These settings do not eliminate all SDK data processing.

Google AdMob may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to serve ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. IP addresses can indicate a general location. We do not supply Quran text, searches, notes, precise GPS coordinates or feedback to the advertising service. We have not added a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Where required, privacy choices are handled by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads are requested only when UMP permits requests. When applicable, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to review your choices. Google may still process data for limited ads, security and fraud prevention under its policies.

Children and families

Religious content is never conditional on viewing an ad. Parents can manage Android permissions and supervise children's use of online features. If you believe a child has sent personal information through Feedback, contact the developer to request removal of that message.

Deletion and choices

Use Settings → Clear All Data or uninstall the app to remove local data, subject to Android storage and backup behavior. Clearing local app data cannot delete feedback already delivered to the developer or data processed by third-party services. For privacy requests, use the app's Feedback form or the developer contact on Google Play.
